Peachblue syncs Google Ads Search, Display, and Video campaigns, including per-asset performance for responsive search ads. Available on Pro plans and up. Read-only access; nothing in your account is ever changed.

## Connect

1. In Peachblue, go to Settings, then Integrations, and click Connect next to Google Ads.
2. Sign in with the Google account that has access to your Google Ads customer accounts and approve read-only ads access.
3. Select the customer accounts to sync. Manager accounts (MCCs) are shown for context but you sync the child accounts that actually contain campaigns.

## What syncs

- Daily performance for Search, Display, and Video campaigns, segmented by network (Search, Display, YouTube)
- Responsive search ads with **per-asset performance**: each headline and description variant with its own impressions and clicks, so analysis can tell you which specific lines win
- Campaign names, types, and status for context

Text-only ads (RSAs) go through a dedicated text analysis path: strategic tagging, a written analysis grounded in which of your variants actually performed, and suggested alternative headlines and descriptions based on your winning patterns. See [creative analysis](/docs/creative-analysis).

## Not yet covered

- **Performance Max**: uses a different asset structure in Google's API; on the roadmap
- **Shopping and App campaigns**: excluded by design, as they carry no analyzable creative content

## Sync schedule

Initial backfill on connect, then daily automatic sync, with manual sync available from the Data Hub.
