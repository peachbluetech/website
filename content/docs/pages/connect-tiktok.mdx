Connecting TikTok Ads works like every Peachblue integration: OAuth sign-in, pick your advertiser accounts, done. Read-only; Peachblue never modifies campaigns.

## Connect

1. In Peachblue, go to Settings, then Integrations, and click Connect next to TikTok.
2. Sign in with your TikTok for Business account and approve access.
3. Select the advertiser accounts to sync and confirm.

## What syncs

- Daily ad-level performance: spend, impressions, clicks, conversions, and revenue where reported
- Campaign and ad group context
- Creative assets, mirrored to Peachblue's storage for durable analysis
- Creative metadata used for grouping the same creative across ads

TikTok data lands in the same unified model as Meta, Google Ads, and Amazon DSP, so cross-platform views (Performance, Creative Library, Agent Peach answers) treat it as a first-class citizen, and the same creative used on both Meta and TikTok is recognized as one creative.

## Sync schedule

Initial backfill on connect, then a daily automatic sync. Manual sync is available from the Data Hub, and sync history with per-advertiser status lives in the connection's details in Settings.

For what Peachblue does with this data once connected, see the [TikTok integration overview](/integrations/tiktok).
