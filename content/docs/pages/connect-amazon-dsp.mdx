Peachblue syncs Amazon DSP campaign performance, orders, line items, and creative assets, and powers per-order flight pacing and client reporting on top. Available on Scale plans and up; [Reports and pacing](/docs/reports-and-pacing) are Power and up. Read-only; your DSP campaigns are never modified.

## Connect

1. In Peachblue, go to Settings, then Integrations, and click Connect next to Amazon.
2. Sign in with your Amazon Ads account and approve access.
3. Select the advertising profile to sync (one per marketplace or entity) and confirm.

## What syncs

- Daily creative-level DSP performance: spend, impressions, clicks, viewability, video metrics, purchases, detail page views, add-to-carts, and new-to-brand metrics
- Orders and line items with budgets and flight dates, which power [pacing](/docs/reports-and-pacing)
- Creative assets, retrieved and mirrored automatically so DSP creatives get the same [AI analysis](/docs/creative-analysis) as your social ads
- Advertiser names and IDs, which power the per-client views in agency workspaces

## Amazon DSP specifics worth knowing

Amazon's DSP reporting behaves differently from social platforms, and Peachblue works within those rules rather than papering over them:

- **Amazon's attribution rules.** DSP credits conversions to clicks and views on a last-touch model, 14 days by default, with a shorter signal-based window for views on ads in Amazon's store since January 1, 2026. Peachblue labels DSP conversion metrics accordingly instead of mixing windows across platforms.
- **Up to 60 days of backfill.** When you connect, you choose how far back to pull: 7, 30, or 60 days of DSP reporting. From then on your history builds forward from daily syncs. Connect early; your history compounds.
- **Multiple line items per creative.** The same creative often runs through several line items on the same day. Peachblue aggregates these correctly so daily numbers match what you would compute from the console.

## Sync schedule

Initial backfill on connect (in windowed chunks, matching Amazon's report limits), then daily automatic sync. Manual sync is available from the Data Hub. Backfills are rate-limited to protect your API quota.

## For agencies

Agency workspaces get a client switcher that scopes Performance, Creative Library, Reports, Brand Intel, and Agent Peach to one DSP advertiser at a time, plus per-client reporting with your agency margin applied. See [Reports and pacing](/docs/reports-and-pacing).

For what Peachblue does with this data once connected, see the [Amazon DSP integration overview](/integrations/amazon-dsp).
