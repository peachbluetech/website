# Competitor truth

Verified facts about competitors. Every fact carries a verification date.
Comparison pages must be regenerated only after re-verifying against live
pricing pages — third-party articles are routinely months stale. Be fair:
every competitor entry includes what they are genuinely good at.

## Motion (motionapp.com) — verified 2026-08-12

- Pricing: Starter $750/mo (up to $50k/mo ad spend), Pro $1,050/mo (over
  $50k), Growth custom (over $125k). Demo-led sales. Free plan exists as a
  limited entry point. Raised its Starter price from $250 to $750 during 2026.
- Platforms: Meta, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn. No Google Ads search/display,
  no Amazon.
- Strengths (concede honestly): the category-defining incumbent; strongest
  community and education in the space (Thumbstop newsletter 50k+ subs,
  summits, courses); attribution integrations (Northbeam, GA4); benchmark
  reports from $14B+ analyzed spend.
- Fit: strong choice for teams spending $50k+/mo on paid social who want the
  community and services layer.

## Atria (tryatria.com) — verified 2026-08-12

- Pricing: Core $129/mo annual (~$161 monthly), Plus $479/mo annual (~$599
  monthly), Business $959/mo annual (~$1,199 monthly), Enterprise custom.
  AI credits do not roll over.
- Platforms: Meta + TikTok only.
- Strengths: polished creative research UX; Raya/Radar AI strategists; strong
  founder-led community.
- Fit: Meta+TikTok-only teams that want creative research plus generation.

## MagicBrief — verified 2026-08-12

- Shut down July 31, 2026 following the Canva acquisition. Team and tech
  became Canva Grow (2.0 unveiled June 2026): create/launch/optimize inside
  Canva for Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn; bundled with Canva plans (Pro $18,
  Business $25 per user).
- Canva Grow strengths: excellent for SMB/generalist marketers already in
  Canva; tight create-to-launch loop.
- Canva Grow gaps vs MagicBrief: not a media-buyer analytics tool; no deep
  creative-performance analysis, no agency reporting.

## Foreplay (foreplay.co) — verified 2026-08-12

- Pricing: Basic $59/mo ($49 annual), Workflow $175 ($149), Agency $459
  ($389), Enterprise custom. $20 per extra seat.
- What it is: swipe-file-first ad research (200M+ ad discovery library),
  briefs, and Lens creative analytics on top. MCP included in every tier —
  exposes their competitor-ad discovery library to Claude/ChatGPT (not your
  own ad-account performance data).
- Strengths: best-in-class swipe file and inspiration workflow; bootstrapped
  and product-led; Chrome extension.
- Fit: creative strategists whose main job is research and briefs.

## Madgicx (madgicx.com) — verified 2026-08-12

- Pricing: gated behind login; spend-bracketed single plan, third-party
  sources put entry around $49-99/mo rising with spend. Tracking add-on
  $49/mo.
- What it is: Meta-centric automation/optimization suite with creative
  reporting modules.
- Strengths: broad Meta ops automation in one tool at low entry price.
- Fit: Meta-only advertisers who want automation more than creative depth.

## Segwise (segwise.ai) — verified 2026-08-12

- Pricing: Growth $499/mo (up to $250k spend; $250/mo startup discount under
  $50k), Pro $1,699/mo, Enterprise custom.
- What it is: AI creative analytics + generation; multimodal tagging mapped
  to performance. Strong in mobile gaming/apps (MMP + ad-network
  integrations). Meta, Google, TikTok.
- Strengths: deep tag-to-performance analytics; good for app marketers.
- Gaps: no Amazon DSP, no Reddit intel, no agency pacing/margin reports.

## rule1.ai — verified 2026-08-12

- Pricing: Growth ~€149/mo (5 seats, aimed at Meta spenders €10k+/mo);
  standalone MCP server ~€20/mo.
- What it is: agentic creative analysis — frame-by-frame video, custom
  rules, weekly briefs. Meta, TikTok, Google, Triple Whale integration.
- Strengths: cheap, agent-forward, fast-moving.
- Gaps: young product, Meta-centric, no Amazon DSP, no agency features.

## GetCrux (getcrux.ai) — verified 2026-08-12

- Pricing: Starter $999/mo billed annually (under $1M spend), higher tiers
  custom. Free plan: 1 account, limited questions.
- What it is: creative tagging + predictive scoring + fatigue detection
  across the widest platform list in the cohort (incl. Pinterest, Snap,
  AppLovin, TradeDesk).
- Fit: mid-market/enterprise multi-channel teams.

## Enterprise DSP creative analytics (context, not direct rivals)

- Skai (Creative Center), VidMob (predictive creative scoring; Amazon DSP +
  TTD + Yahoo + DV360), CreativeX (creative quality governance). All
  enterprise, sales-led. This is why our claim is phrased "for agencies at
  self-serve pricing" — see voice.md claim rules.

## DSP ops tools (adjacent)

- Pacvue: ~$500/mo + 2-3% of spend; DSP campaign ops incumbent; campaign
  metrics, not creative intelligence.
- Perpetua/Flywheel, Quartile, Intentwise, Adbrew, m19: DSP automation/BI at
  various tiers; none do AI creative analysis.
