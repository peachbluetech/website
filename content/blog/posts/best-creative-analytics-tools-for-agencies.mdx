The best creative analytics tools for agencies in 2026 are Peachblue, Motion, Superads, Segwise, Atria, Foreplay, Madgicx, Hawky, Triple Whale, and Vidmob. For an agency the deciding question is not the dashboard but the roster: whether one login keeps many clients' data apart, whether the tool covers every platform your clients buy on, Amazon DSP included, and whether the price scales by client, by seat, or by ad spend.

Every price below was checked on the vendor's own pricing page on October 21, 2026, with monthly billing unless noted. We make Peachblue, so it is on the list; every other entry says who that tool is genuinely right for.

## What should an agency look for in a creative analytics tool?

Five things, and only the last two show up on a brand team's checklist:

1. **Client separation.** One login, many clients, with each client's data, reports, and AI answers kept apart. Ask whether the tool has a workspace per client or just folders and filters.
2. **Platform coverage across the roster.** Most tools are built around Meta and TikTok. If any client buys Google, LinkedIn, mobile app networks, or Amazon DSP, check that platform by name.
3. **Client-facing output.** Live share links, PDF exports, view-only guests, and whether your agency margin can be applied to the numbers a client sees.
4. **How the price scales.** By ad spend (Motion, Superads, Segwise, Madgicx), by brand (Hawky, Vidmob), by plan with seat and account limits (Atria, Foreplay), or by roster (Peachblue's Agency plan). Past a handful of clients, the pricing model matters more than the entry price.
5. **AI that respects the roster.** Chat and MCP access to Claude or ChatGPT are now common. The agency question is whether a question about one client can only see that client.

## 1. Peachblue: best for agencies whose clients buy beyond Meta

Peachblue is a creative intelligence platform with an agency mode: a client switcher scopes the app, from the creative library to reports, Intelligence, Brand Intel, and AI answers, to one client at a time, where a client is an Amazon DSP advertiser or a Meta ad account. Every creative is analyzed on 31 dimensions with a written read and copy suggestions, and perceptual fingerprinting counts the same asset once across ads and platforms, so a video running in six ad sets on two platforms is judged as one creative. Intelligence finds what a client's winners share, and the Next Creative Brief turns it into concepts, proven hooks, and a generation-ready prompt block you can share with the client or a creator by link.

What sets it apart for agencies is coverage and the reporting layer. It syncs Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, and Amazon DSP, and it is the only creative analytics platform for agencies at self-serve pricing that covers Amazon DSP, with per-order flight pacing (pace, days remaining, blended CPM against your CPM goal) and supplier breakdowns. Client reports apply your agency margin to spend and every spend-derived metric, while pacing stays measured against the raw budget, and Brand Intel monitors Reddit for each client's brand and competitors. Agent Peach answers questions in the app, and the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) puts the same tools in Claude, with every call scoped to the client you have selected and a roster view when none is. It is read-only: nothing it does can change a campaign.

**Pricing:** Starter $79/mo (Meta and TikTok), Pro $199 (adds Google Ads and MCP), Scale $499 (adds Amazon DSP), Power $799 (adds client-ready reports and pacing), and Agency priced to your client roster (multi-client workspaces, margin on reports, 25 seats). Annual billing gives two months free, and Starter and Pro have a 7-day trial. Full details on the [pricing page](/pricing).

**Choose Peachblue if** your clients buy beyond Meta and TikTok, especially Amazon DSP, and you want creative analysis, pacing, and client reporting in one place.

## 2. Motion: best for social-first agencies that want unlimited seats

Motion is the category incumbent and the most social-native pick here. Every plan includes unlimited seats and unlimited ad accounts, which suits an agency with a large team, and reports can be shared with clients as live links, with unlimited view-only guests from Pro. It covers Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn, includes MCP data access from Starter, and runs an AI layer it calls Runneth inside Slack. Motion is known for its education ecosystem (newsletter, courses, events), and its partner program pays agencies a revenue share on clients they refer. Its pricing page lists no agency plan or client workspaces.

**Pricing:** Starter $999/mo for up to $50k of monthly ad spend (it was $750 when we checked in September); Pro and Growth, for higher spend, on request. No annual price is published.

**Choose Motion if** your clients are social-first, your team lives in Slack, and unlimited seats matter more to you than per-client separation.

## 3. Superads: best for low-cost reporting across several clients

Superads is the lowest published entry for agencies whose main job is creative reporting. Its one plan, Professional, includes unlimited users and multi-account reports, with boards organized by client, view-only guests, and scheduled reports to Slack or email. It adds AI tagging, a 0 to 100 score per creative, an Ask AI assistant, and competitor inspiration, across Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, and LinkedIn. The free plan has closed to new signups; a 14-day trial replaces it.

**Pricing:** $150/mo, or $125/mo billed yearly, for up to $100k of combined monthly ad spend, plus $100/mo for each extra $100k. Enterprise on request.

**Choose Superads if** you want shareable creative reports across several clients at the lowest published price, on social and search.

## 4. Segwise: best for mobile app and game clients

Segwise has the widest network coverage on this list, built for app and game advertisers: 15+ ad networks, including AppLovin, Unity, Mintegral, and Moloco, plus MMP integrations. Every plan includes every feature: multimodal AI tagging (playable ads included), a creative strategy agent, generation of statics, video, playables, and CTV ads, fatigue tracking, competitor insights, Studio View for multiple brands, and unlimited seats and view-only users. Its Enterprise tier is pitched at agencies.

**Pricing:** Growth $499/mo, or $449/mo billed annually, for up to $250k of monthly ad spend; Startup $250/mo for eligible teams under $50k; Pro and Enterprise on request. 7-day trial.

**Choose Segwise if** your clients are app and game advertisers buying mobile networks with an MMP.

## 5. Atria: best for Meta and TikTok agencies that also want generation

Atria pairs analysis with research and generation for Meta and TikTok: AI tagging, an ad library, brand tracking, generation of concepts, scripts, copy, and image ads, a 24/7 AI strategist called Radar, and MCP access on every plan. For an agency the plan limits decide it: Core connects 5 ad accounts, Plus 10, and Business unlimited, and only Business adds guest invites and custom metrics. Seats are 5, 8, and 15, with more at $20 each.

**Pricing:** Core $159/mo, Plus $599, Business $1,199 billed monthly ($129, $479, and $959 billed annually). MCP is capped at 600 calls a month on Core.

**Choose Atria if** your roster runs on Meta and TikTok and you want research, generation, and analysis in one tool.

## 6. Foreplay: best for competitor research for clients

Foreplay is the strongest competitor-research tool here and the odd one out: its core is swipe files, ad library discovery, and Spyder competitor tracking. Lens adds analytics on your own Meta and TikTok accounts. The Agency plan is sized for a small roster: 10 users, Lens on 10 brands, and competitor tracking on 50 (unlimited on annual billing), with MCP access to Claude and ChatGPT. Usage and ad spend are unlimited on every plan.

**Pricing:** Basic $59/mo, Workflow $175, Agency $459 billed monthly ($49, $149, and $389 billed annually). 7-day trial.

**Choose Foreplay if** competitor research is the deliverable, or as a research layer beside a performance tool.

## 7. Madgicx: best for Meta agencies that want automation

Madgicx is Meta automation first and creative analytics second. It optimizes Meta ads only, with an AI Marketer that prepares actions you approve in one click, automation rules, and a Facebook MCP that can change status, budgets, and bids from Claude or ChatGPT. Creative Insights breaks down every creative and flags fatigue, and One-Click Report pulls Meta, Google Ads, TikTok, Shopify, and Klaviyo into reports agencies share with clients by link or PDF.

**Pricing:** from $45/mo, priced by monthly ad spend, per Madgicx's own figure (the pricing page shows the price inside the app). 7-day free trial.

**Choose Madgicx if** your clients are Meta-heavy ecommerce brands and you want automation and client reports in the same tool.

## 8. Hawky: best for agencies that want AI agents to act on accounts

Hawky is an agent platform with creative analytics underneath: element-level creative dashboards, auto tagging, competitor tracking, a Copilot for plain-language questions, and shareable reports across Meta, Google, and TikTok. Its top tier adds agents that move bids and budgets and pause on anomalies. Pricing is per brand with unlimited users, which suits an agency with a large team on a handful of brands.

**Pricing:** on request; three tiers (Dashboards, Ask, Auto-pilot), priced per brand.

**Choose Hawky if** you want AI agents that act on client accounts, not only report on them.

## 9. Triple Whale: best for DTC agencies tying creative to revenue

Triple Whale is an ecommerce attribution platform with creative tools on top: Triple Pixel attribution, Moby AI as an analyst on every plan, and, on Automate, tools that find what makes your best ads work and generate variations to test. Enterprise adds marketing mix modeling and incrementality testing. For an agency it fits when the client conversation is revenue first and creative second.

**Pricing:** free plan; Foundation, Automate, and Enterprise through a walkthrough or sales.

**Choose Triple Whale if** your clients are DTC ecommerce brands and attribution is the question you were hired to answer.

## 10. Vidmob: best for enterprise brands across many channels

Vidmob is the enterprise option and the only other tool here that covers Amazon: 13 channels, including Amazon Sponsored Ads, Amazon DSP, DV360, and The Trade Desk. It scores creative before and during flight against brand and platform criteria, breaks each asset into elements (talent, tone, pacing, product presence) in reports built to show clients, and includes an AI assistant called Maddie. Pricing is per brand, by annual impressions, with agency terms on request.

**Pricing:** on request; packages sized by impressions per brand.

**Choose Vidmob if** your clients are large brands that need pre-flight scoring and brand compliance across many channels.

## Why do agencies running Amazon DSP pick Peachblue?

Because the agency work on DSP is pacing and reporting, and Peachblue does both next to the creative analysis. Three things no other self-serve tool on this list offers:

- **Amazon DSP in the same account as social and search.** Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, and Amazon DSP sync into one Peachblue account, and the same creative is matched across platforms. Vidmob is the only other tool here that covers DSP, at enterprise, quote-only pricing.
- **Pacing per order, every day.** Budget, spend to date, pace, days remaining, and blended CPM against the CPM goal you set, so an underdelivering flight shows up in week one. The method is in our [Amazon DSP pacing guide](/blog/amazon-dsp-pacing-guide).
- **Client reports with your margin built in.** Margin applies per client to spend and every spend-derived metric, server-side, and the all-clients view never applies it. None of the other nine pricing pages lists margin handling.

In Claude, the same holds: ask about one client and the answer only sees that client. How that works next to Amazon's own connector is in [Amazon Ads MCP in Claude](/blog/amazon-ads-mcp-claude).
