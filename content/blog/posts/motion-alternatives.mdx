## What actually changed at Motion

Motion repositioned upmarket during 2026: the entry tier went from $250 to $750, the Pro tier got a public $1,050 price, and the positioning shifted to "the AI brain for marketing," with a dedicated AI workspace per account. A limited free plan exists as an entry point. The platform list is unchanged: Meta, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, with Northbeam and GA4 attribution integrations. Still no Google Ads, still no Amazon.

None of that is a criticism. It is a strategy, and it tells you exactly who Motion wants: teams on paid social who want the full Motion ecosystem attached to their software. The question is whether that is you.

## Who should actually stay on Motion

Two honest reasons to stay or sign up anyway:

1. **The ecosystem.** Motion's education layer, the Thumbstop newsletter, the summits, the courses, the live streams, is the best in the category, and nobody on this list matches it. Worth knowing: most of it is free to everyone, whether or not you pay for the software.
2. **Attribution integrations.** If Northbeam or GA4 attribution inside your creative analytics tool is a hard requirement, Motion has it and most alternatives do not.

If neither of those describes your situation, here is the field.
