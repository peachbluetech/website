Full disclosure before anything else: I build Peachblue, one of the tools on this list, so read accordingly. The reason to write a fair version anyway is that this category's comparison content is uniformly self-serving (every ranked page puts its author first), and the honest map is more useful to you and, frankly, to us. Every price below was re-verified against live pricing pages on September 2, 2026, which matters more than usual right now: two of the incumbents repriced in the last year and most third-party articles still cite the old numbers.

The category in one paragraph: creative analytics tools connect to your ad accounts, analyze the creatives themselves (not just the campaigns), and tell you which ones work and why. They differ on three axes that should drive your choice: which platforms they cover, whether they analyze your own ads or competitors' ads, and whether the analysis layer is tags and dashboards or something you can interrogate.

## Motion

The category incumbent. Starter is $750 per month for up to $50k monthly ad spend, Pro $1,200, Growth custom, all demo-gated with unlimited seats. Motion is known for its education ecosystem (the Thumbstop newsletter, courses, events, a genuine community that exists whether or not you buy) and its Slack-native workflow: reports, briefs, and alerts arrive where the team already lives, with MCP data access now included from Starter. Its center of gravity is Meta.

**Choose Motion if** your team is Meta-centric, lives in Slack, and values the surrounding education and services layer as part of the purchase.

## Atria

Meta plus TikTok creative analytics with brand tracking. Core $129 per month, Plus $479, Business $959, Enterprise custom, with credits metering AI usage and seats added at $20. Two honest strengths: the Core tier is one of the cheapest real entries in the category, and as of this verification every plan includes free unlimited MCP access with ChatGPT, Claude, and Cursor support named. The platform ceiling is the trade: two ad platforms, so Google or Amazon spend lives outside it.

**Choose Atria if** you run Meta and TikTok only and want brand-tracking features and MCP access at a low entry price.

## Foreplay

The strongest competitor-ad research and swipe-file product in the category, and the odd one out on this list: its core job is analyzing everyone else's ads (discovery, briefs, brand spying), not your own performance data. Basic is $59 per month and now includes Lens, its analytics layer, plus MCP; Workflow $175 and Agency $459 scale the brand counts. If your bottleneck is inspiration and creative research rather than performance measurement, it is excellent, and plenty of teams run it alongside a performance tool.

**Choose Foreplay if** competitor research and swipe files are the job, or as a complement to any tool below.

## Segwise

Creative analytics with a mobile-first tilt: alongside Meta, Google, and TikTok it connects Unity, ironSource, Mintegral, and the MMPs (AppsFlyer, Adjust, Singular, Kochava), which nobody else on this list does. Growth is $499 per month up to $250k spend, with a $250 startup rate for teams under $50k, and Pro $1,699. Tagging, a creative strategy agent, fatigue tracking, unlimited seats.

**Choose Segwise if** you advertise mobile apps or games and need creative analytics that speaks MMP.

## rule1

The budget entry: EUR 149 per month for the full product with 5 seats, and a EUR 20 per month MCP-only plan that pipes your ad data into AI tools without the dashboard. Twenty tagging dimensions and frame-by-frame video analysis, Meta-centric. One correction offered with a smile, since their comparison pages claim to be the only creative analytics platform with an MCP server: as of this writing Peachblue, Motion, Atria, and Foreplay all ship MCP. The EUR 20 plan is still a genuinely clever wedge.

**Choose rule1 if** you want the cheapest path to tagged creative data, in a dashboard or over MCP.

## GetCrux

An AI creative strategist (YC W24) that analyzes winning ads and generates concepts across Meta, Google, and TikTok, leaning further into ideation and generation than the pure analytics tools. Pricing is on request; their public pricing page was returning an error at verification time, so treat any number you read elsewhere as unconfirmed.

**Choose GetCrux if** you want analysis and generation from one vendor and are comfortable with quote-based pricing.

## uplifted

A creative asset management platform that grew an analytics layer: Core at $249 per month is the AI-organized library (tagged, clipped, searchable), Scale at $499 adds ad performance and an AI agent. If your team's daily pain is asset chaos across Drive and Frame.io, this is the only tool on the list aimed at that problem first.

**Choose uplifted if** asset organization is the primary pain and performance analytics the secondary one.

## How to decide

- **Meta-only team that wants community and services around the tool**: Motion.
- **Meta + TikTok on a modest budget**: Atria, or Peachblue at [$79](/pricing) if you may add platforms later.
- **Competitor research and swipe files**: Foreplay, possibly alongside a performance tool.
- **Mobile apps and games**: Segwise, nothing else here speaks MMP.
- **Cheapest possible tagged data**: rule1.
- **Asset chaos first, analytics second**: uplifted.
- **Multi-platform, Amazon DSP, agencies, or AI-queryable data**: Peachblue.

Also worth knowing: MagicBrief, formerly a major player here, [shut down in July 2026](/blog/magicbrief-alternatives), and if you arrived comparing Motion specifically, the [Motion alternatives](/blog/motion-alternatives) page goes deeper. Whichever way you go, buy against the axis that actually constrains you: platform coverage is the one that cannot be worked around later.
