## What does a creative analytics tool do?

A creative analytics tool connects to your ad accounts and judges the creative itself, not just the campaign: which images, videos, hooks, and copy drive results, and why. Most tag each creative by format, hook, message, and other traits, score its performance, and look for the patterns your winners share. The better ones turn those patterns into what to make next.

## How do you choose a creative analytics tool?

Four questions decide it:

1. **Which platforms do you buy on?** Most tools are built around Meta and TikTok. If you also buy Google Ads, LinkedIn, mobile app networks, or Amazon DSP, check each one by name. Platform coverage is the one gap you cannot work around later.
2. **Your ads or everyone else's?** Some tools analyze your own performance; others, like Foreplay, are built for researching competitors' ads. Many teams use one of each.
3. **Tags or answers?** A dashboard of tags tells you what your ads contain. An analysis tells you why one beat another and what to try next.
4. **How does the price scale?** By ad spend, by brand, by seat, or by plan. Check the price at your real spend, not the entry tier.

## 1. Peachblue: best for teams that buy beyond Meta

Peachblue analyzes every creative on 31 dimensions with a written read and copy suggestions, scores it, and counts the same asset once across ads and platforms, so a video re-cropped for Reels is still judged as one creative. Intelligence finds what your winners share, and the Next Creative Brief turns it into concepts, proven hooks, and a generation-ready prompt block for the next round. Dashboards flag fatigue against each creative's own best week and show wasted spend. Agent Peach answers questions in the app, the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) puts the same data in Claude, and Brand Intel tracks what Reddit says about your brand and competitors. It covers Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, and Amazon DSP, and it is the only creative analytics platform at self-serve pricing that covers Amazon DSP.

**Pricing:** Starter $79/mo (Meta and TikTok), Pro $199 (adds Google Ads and MCP), Scale $499 (adds Amazon DSP), Power $799 (adds reports and pacing), and Agency priced to your client roster. Annual billing gives two months free; Starter and Pro have a 7-day trial. [See pricing](/pricing).

**Choose Peachblue if** you want to know what to make next, not just what happened, on Meta and TikTok alone or across every platform you buy.
