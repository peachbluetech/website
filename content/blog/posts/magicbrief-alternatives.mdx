Full disclosure before anything else: I am the founder of Peachblue, one of the tools on this list. I have tried to be straight about who each tool fits, including when that is not us. All prices were verified against live pricing pages on August 12, 2026, because a lot of the comparison content out there is months stale.

## What happened to MagicBrief?

Canva acquired MagicBrief in mid-2025 and shut the standalone product down on July 31, 2026. The team and technology became Canva Grow, which Canva expanded into Canva Grow 2.0 at Cannes in June 2026. Grow is an ad creation, launch, and optimization layer inside Canva itself, covering Meta, TikTok, and LinkedIn, and it ships inside Canva plans (Pro at $18 and Business at $25 per user per month) rather than as a standalone product.

That answers the first question most former customers have: there is a successor, but it is a different kind of product. MagicBrief was built for people whose job is ads. Canva Grow is built for people whose job includes ads.

## What did you actually use MagicBrief for?

Your replacement depends on which half of MagicBrief you lived in:

1. **Research and swipe files.** Saving competitor ads, building inspiration boards, briefing creators from winning examples.
2. **Creative analytics.** Connecting your Meta account and understanding which of your own creatives performed and why.

No single tool on this list is the best at both. MagicBrief itself was stronger on research than analytics, which shaped who loved it.

## Canva Grow: the official successor

If your team already lives in Canva and your ads workload is creating and launching, Canva Grow is the path of least resistance. It inherits MagicBrief's insight engine, connects to Meta, TikTok, and LinkedIn, and costs nothing extra beyond the Canva plan you may already pay for.

What it is not: a media buyer's analytics tool. There is no deep creative-performance analysis, no agency reporting, and no way to interrogate why one concept beat another beyond surface metrics. If MagicBrief was your research or analytics tool rather than your creation tool, Grow will feel like a downgrade.

**Choose Canva Grow if** you are an SMB or generalist marketer, you already pay for Canva, and creation-to-launch speed matters more than analysis depth.

## Foreplay: the research replacement

For swipe files and creative research, Foreplay is the strongest tool standing. Its discovery library is the largest in the category, the save-and-organize workflow is the most refined, and the Chrome extension makes collecting references frictionless. Basic starts at $49 per month billed annually ($59 monthly), with team workflow at $149 to $175 and agency tiers at $389 to $459. Creative analytics exists through its Lens feature, but it is a bolt-on to a research-first product, not the core.

**Choose Foreplay if** the thing you miss most is the swipe file. It is the closest one-to-one replacement for MagicBrief's research half.

## Motion: the analytics incumbent, now firmly upmarket

Motion is the category-defining creative analytics product, and it earned that position: strong reporting, a large education and community layer, and attribution integrations. But Motion repriced in 2026. Entry is now $750 per month for accounts up to $50k monthly spend, with the Pro tier at $1,050. If you were on MagicBrief partly because Motion felt expensive at its old $250 entry price, that gap has tripled.

**Choose Motion if** you spend $50k a month or more on paid social, your platforms are Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn, and you want the community, courses, and services layer as much as the software.

## Atria: research plus generation for Meta and TikTok

Atria combines creative research, analytics, and AI generation with an agent layer (Raya and Radar) on top. Core is $129 per month billed annually. Two honest caveats: the platform covers Meta and TikTok only, and the mid tier jumped roughly 80% in 2026 to $479 to $599 per month, so check current pricing against your growth plans before committing.

**Choose Atria if** you run Meta and TikTok only and want research, generation, and analytics in one place.

## How to decide in five minutes

- **You mostly saved and organized competitor ads:** Foreplay.
- **You mostly made ads and launched them:** Canva Grow.
- **You mostly analyzed your own ad performance, spend under $50k/mo:** Peachblue.
- **You mostly analyzed your own ad performance, spend over $50k/mo on paid social:** Motion, with Peachblue worth a trial first at a tenth of the entry price.
- **You are Meta plus TikTok only and want generation built in:** Atria.

One last practical note: MagicBrief's export window closed with the shutdown, but if you kept CSV exports or board screenshots, every tool above can rebuild from them faster than starting cold. Whatever you pick, migrate your naming conventions and winning-ad archive first; the tool matters less than keeping that institutional memory intact.
