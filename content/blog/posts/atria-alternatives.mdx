The best Atria alternatives in 2026 are Peachblue, Motion, Foreplay, Superads, Segwise, GetCrux, and rule1. Atria bundles research, generation, and analytics for Meta and TikTok, so the right alternative depends on which of those jobs you need done better, and on whether you buy anywhere beyond Meta and TikTok.

Disclosure up front: we make Peachblue, one of the tools below. Every price here was checked on the vendor's own pricing page on October 21 or 23, 2026, with monthly billing unless noted, and every entry says who that tool is genuinely right for, Atria included.

## Why do teams look for Atria alternatives?

Mostly for platform coverage, the price of the middle tiers, and limits that bite as an account or a client list grows.

- **Meta and TikTok only.** Atria's performance analytics covers those two platforms. Google Ads and Amazon DSP spend stays outside the tool.
- **The middle tiers.** Core is $159 a month, but Plus is $599 and Business $1,199 ($129, $479, and $959 billed annually). Core connects 5 ad accounts and Plus 10; Business is unlimited.
- **Plan limits.** Core analyzes up to $500k of ad spend and Plus up to $1M; MCP on Core is capped at 600 calls a month, with extra calls costing AI credits.
- **Agency needs.** Business adds guest invites, but when we checked, Atria's site listed no multi-client workspaces or client reporting.

## Who should stay on Atria

Teams that run only Meta and TikTok and want one tool to research, make, and judge ads. Atria pairs an ad library and auto-tagging with generation of concepts, scripts, copy, and image ads, plus AI agents that grade each ad and suggest fixes. You can start free with 1,000 credits and no card. If that combined workflow is the point, few tools match it at $159. Our [Peachblue vs Atria](/blog/peachblue-vs-atria) comparison goes dimension by dimension.

## What did you use Atria for?

- **Analyzing your own ads:** Peachblue, Motion, Superads, or rule1.

## Peachblue: analysis that covers every platform you buy

Peachblue connects to Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, and Amazon DSP and gives every creative a written analysis across 31 dimensions, with copy suggestions and a composite score. Perceptual fingerprinting recognizes the same asset across ads and platforms, so one video is judged as one creative wherever it runs.

What it adds for a team leaving Atria:

- **What to make next, from your own data.** Intelligence finds what your winners share and turns it into a Next Creative Brief: concepts, proven hooks from your own headlines, and a generation-ready prompt block for the image or video tool you already use.
- **Your ad data in Claude.** Agent Peach answers questions in the app, and the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) puts the same 23 tools into Claude. It is the only creative analytics MCP that serves your own cross-platform performance data, Amazon DSP included.
- **Brand Intel.** Reddit monitoring for your brand, competitors, and topics, with sentiment and an editorial brief.
- **Agency infrastructure.** Multi-client workspaces, per-client reports with agency margin, and Amazon DSP flight pacing. It is the only creative analytics platform for agencies at self-serve pricing that covers Amazon DSP.

Plans: Starter $79 (Meta and TikTok), Pro $199 (adds Google Ads, Intelligence, Brand Intel, and MCP), Scale $499 (adds Amazon DSP), Power $799 (adds client-ready reports and pacing), and Agency priced to your client roster. Starter and Pro have a 7-day trial. Full matrix on [pricing](/pricing).

What Atria is known for that matters here: research and generation in the same tool as analysis.

**Choose Peachblue if** you buy beyond Meta and TikTok, run a client roster, or want the next brief written from your own winners.

## Motion: the incumbent, with unlimited seats

Motion includes unlimited seats and ad accounts on every plan, shares reports as live links, runs an AI layer inside Slack, and includes MCP access from Starter. It adds YouTube and LinkedIn to Meta and TikTok, and its education ecosystem (newsletter, courses, events) is the best in the category, much of it free to everyone. Our [Motion vs Atria](/blog/motion-vs-atria) page compares the two directly.

**Pricing:** Starter $999 a month for up to $50k of monthly ad spend; Pro and Growth on request.

**Choose Motion if** your team is social-first, lives in Slack, and values the education around the tool.

## Foreplay: if research was the point

Many Atria users came for the ad library. Foreplay is the strongest research tool in the category: swipe files, ad library discovery, and competitor tracking, with Lens analytics on your own Meta and TikTok ads from the Workflow plan and MCP from Workflow too.

**Pricing:** Basic $59 a month (research only), Workflow $175, Agency $459 ($49, $149, and $389 billed annually). 7-day trial.

**Choose Foreplay if** research and briefs are the job, often beside a performance tool.

## Superads: shareable reporting for less

One plan with unlimited users, AI tagging, a score per creative, an Ask AI assistant, and reports shared as live links or scheduled to Slack and email. It covers Google Ads and LinkedIn as well as Meta and TikTok. The free plan has closed to new signups; a 14-day trial replaces it.

**Pricing:** $150 a month, or $125 billed yearly, for up to $100k of monthly ad spend, plus $100 a month per extra $100k.

**Choose Superads if** clear reporting across social and search matters more than generation.

## Segwise: built for apps and games

Segwise covers 15+ ad networks plus MMP integrations, with every feature on every plan: multimodal tagging, a creative strategy agent, generation of statics, video, playables, and CTV ads, fatigue tracking, and unlimited seats.

**Pricing:** Growth $499 a month ($449 billed annually) up to $250k of monthly ad spend; Startup $250 for eligible teams under $50k.

**Choose Segwise if** you advertise apps or games on mobile networks.

## GetCrux: analysis and generation from one vendor

GetCrux combines creative insights, AI tagging, competitor insights, a Copilot, and credits for generating image and video ads, with publishing built in. Starter covers Meta, Google, TikTok, and LinkedIn; higher plans reach 12 platforms and add MCP. Sold through a demo, with no trial.

**Pricing:** Starter $1,500 a month for up to $100k of monthly ad spend and 2 ad accounts; Professional and Enterprise on request.

**Choose GetCrux if** you spend heavily and want one vendor for analysis and ad production.

## rule1: low-cost AI analysis for Meta

rule1's Growth plan explains why your winners work across 20 creative dimensions, with shareable reports and 5 seats; a separate plan offers MCP access alone. It integrates with Meta, TikTok, Google, and Triple Whale.

**Pricing:** EUR 149 a month for Growth, EUR 20 for MCP only. 7-day trial.

**Choose rule1 if** you want AI analysis of Meta ads on a small budget.
