The best Superads alternatives in 2026 are Peachblue, Motion, Atria, Segwise, Madgicx, rule1, and Foreplay. Superads is a good low-cost creative reporting tool, so the right replacement depends on what pushed you to look: a price that climbs with your ad spend, a platform it does not cover, or wanting an analysis that explains each result and what to make next, not only a report.

Disclosure up front: I am the founder of Peachblue, one of the tools below. Every price here was checked on the vendor's own pricing page on October 21 or 23, 2026, with monthly billing unless noted, and every entry says who that tool is genuinely right for, Superads included.

## Why do teams look for Superads alternatives?

Usually for one of four reasons: the price grows with ad spend, the free plan is gone, a platform is missing, or reports stop being enough.

- **The price scales with spend.** Professional is $150 a month ($125 billed yearly) for up to $100k of monthly ad spend, plus $100 a month for each extra $100k. By our reading of the pricing page, an account spending $500k a month pays $550 a month on monthly billing.
- **No free plan for new signups.** It closed; a 14-day trial replaced it.
- **Platforms.** Superads covers Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, and LinkedIn. Amazon DSP and the mobile app networks are not on the list.
- **Reports, not reasons.** Superads tags, scores, and reports. Teams that want a written read on why one creative beat another, or their ad data inside Claude, look further. Superads' pages did not mention MCP when we checked.

## Who should stay on Superads

Plenty of teams. Unlimited users with no per-seat pricing, boards by client, view-only guests, and reports scheduled to Slack or email make it a strong, inexpensive reporting layer, and it covers LinkedIn, which few tools here do. If clear, shareable creative reporting at the lowest published price is the job, stay.

## What did you use Superads for?

- **Client reporting across accounts:** Peachblue's Agency plan, Motion, or Segwise.
- **Knowing why a creative won and what to make next:** Peachblue, or rule1 on a small Meta budget.
- **The lowest bill:** Madgicx for Meta-first teams, or rule1.
- **Competitor inspiration:** Foreplay, often beside a performance tool.

## Peachblue: from reports to what to make next

Peachblue connects to Meta, TikTok, Google Ads, and Amazon DSP and gives every creative a written analysis across 31 dimensions, with copy suggestions and a composite score. Perceptual fingerprinting recognizes the same asset across ads and platforms, so a video re-cropped for Reels is judged as one creative.

Where Superads stops at the report, Peachblue keeps going:

- **Intelligence and the Next Creative Brief.** Pattern detection finds what your winners share (hooks, tones, formats) and turns it into a brief with concepts, proven hooks from your own headlines, and a prompt block ready for whatever makes your ads.
- **Your ad data in Claude.** Agent Peach answers questions about your accounts in plain language, and the [MCP server](/docs/mcp) puts the same 23 tools into Claude. It is the only creative analytics MCP that serves your own cross-platform performance data, Amazon DSP included.
- **Brand Intel.** Reddit monitoring for your brand, competitors, and topics, with sentiment and an editorial brief you can pull creative angles from.
- **Agency infrastructure.** Multi-client workspaces, per-client reports with agency margin, and Amazon DSP flight pacing. It is the only creative analytics platform for agencies at self-serve pricing that covers Amazon DSP.

Pricing runs by plan, not by ad spend: Starter $79 (Meta and TikTok), Pro $199 (adds Google Ads, Intelligence, Brand Intel, and MCP), Scale $499 (adds Amazon DSP), Power $799 (adds client-ready reports and pacing), and Agency priced to your client roster. Starter and Pro have a 7-day trial. Full matrix on [pricing](/pricing).

What Superads is known for that matters here: LinkedIn coverage and unlimited users on its one plan.

**Choose Peachblue if** you want to know why your creatives win and what to make next, or you buy Google Ads or Amazon DSP alongside social.

## Motion: the incumbent, with unlimited seats

Motion includes unlimited seats and ad accounts on every plan, shares reports as live links, runs an AI layer inside Slack, and includes MCP access from Starter. It covers Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn, and its education ecosystem (newsletter, courses, events) is the best in the category, much of it free to everyone.

**Pricing:** Starter $999 a month for up to $50k of monthly ad spend; Pro and Growth on request. More in [Motion alternatives](/blog/motion-alternatives).

**Choose Motion if** your team is social-first, lives in Slack, and values the education around the tool.

## Atria: research and generation beside analytics

Atria combines AI tagging, an ad library, and generation of concepts, scripts, copy, and image ads for Meta and TikTok, with MCP on every plan (600 calls a month on Core). Core connects 5 ad accounts, Plus 10, Business unlimited.

**Pricing:** Core $159 a month, Plus $599, Business $1,199 ($129, $479, and $959 billed annually).

**Choose Atria if** you run Meta and TikTok only and want research, generation, and analysis in one place.

## Segwise: built for apps and games

Segwise covers 15+ ad networks plus MMP integrations, with every feature on every plan: multimodal tagging, a creative strategy agent, generation, fatigue tracking, a studio view for multiple brands, and unlimited seats and view-only users.

**Pricing:** Growth $499 a month ($449 billed annually) up to $250k of monthly ad spend; Startup $250 for eligible teams under $50k.

**Choose Segwise if** you advertise apps or games on mobile networks.

## Madgicx: Meta automation with reporting

Madgicx optimizes Meta ads only, with automation rules and a Facebook MCP that can change budgets and bids. Its One-Click Report adds Google Ads, TikTok, Shopify, and Klaviyo data and can be shared with clients by link or PDF.

**Pricing:** from $45 a month, priced by monthly ad spend, per Madgicx's own figure. 7-day trial.

**Choose Madgicx if** you are Meta-heavy and want automation and reporting together.

## rule1: low-cost AI analysis for Meta

rule1's Growth plan explains why your winners work across 20 creative dimensions, with shareable reports and 5 seats; a separate plan offers MCP access alone. It integrates with Meta, TikTok, Google, and Triple Whale.

**Pricing:** EUR 149 a month for Growth, EUR 20 for MCP only. 7-day trial.

**Choose rule1 if** you want AI analysis of Meta ads on a small budget.

## Foreplay: if inspiration was the point

If you mostly used Superads for competitor inspiration, Foreplay is the strongest research tool in the category: swipe files, ad library discovery, and competitor tracking. Lens, its analytics on your own Meta and TikTok ads, starts on Workflow.

**Pricing:** Basic $59 a month (research only), Workflow $175, Agency $459 ($49, $149, and $389 billed annually). 7-day trial.

**Choose Foreplay if** research and briefs are the job.
